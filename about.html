About us

From an NTU research lab to a deployable product.

CityWave grew out of years of Reconfigurable Intelligent Surface (RIS) research at NTU — turning lab-validated signal steering into hardware telcos and businesses can actually install.

Our timeline

How we got here

  • 2023 — RIS Research at NTU Multi-year RIS research program at NTU's School of EEE under Prof. Chau Yuen, resulting in 100+ publications and 3 PCT patent filings.
  • 2024 — CityWave Founded CityWave spun out to commercialize the RIS research, led by Yufei Zhao (CEO) and Qihao Lyu (COO & CTO), with NTU faculty as founder and senior advisor.
  • 2025 — First Outdoor Trials Live RIS trials conducted in an underground carpark and outdoor street deployment, recording uplink gains of up to 100% and signal gains of up to 27dB.
  • 2026 — First Commercial Partnerships Signed pilot agreements with Ruitek Pte Ltd (Changi Airport road surface + underground carpark) and Wintek (underground carpark system integration).
NTU Collaboration

Research, patents, and people from NTU

CityWave's core technology originates from Reconfigurable Intelligent Surface research at NTU's School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, led by Prof. Chau Yuen (Founder, IEEE Fellow, 100+ publications, 60,000+ citations) with Prof. Yong Liang Guan (Senior Advisor, Associate Vice President) providing continued guidance.

Three patent applications filed by NTU underpin the technology:

  1. "A reconfigurable intelligent surface system for wireless communications" PCT/SG2024/050160
  2. "A method of processing wireless communication signals for use in radar sensing" PCT/EP2022/085092
  3. "A novel method for radar sensing using communication signals with single carrier preamble and multi-carrier data" PCT/EP2022/054810
