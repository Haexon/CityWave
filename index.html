Why it matters

Base stations don't fit everywhere.

Adding a new base station typically takes 1–2 years and costs US$100k–200k, and can't be deployed in roughly 10% of Singapore's environments — heritage buildings, underground carparks, dense residential blocks. CityWave's RIS panels deploy in 1–2 days at a fraction of the cost, extending usable coverage to 99% of these environments.
